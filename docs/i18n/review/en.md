## Patterns

- Percent spacing: the Hungarian "NN %" spacing was carried over in 8 strings (rows 147, 563, 579, 622, 739, 740 (two places), 961). STYLE.tsv says "55%" with no space; one pass over the English catalog should fix all of them.
- Plural keys: the counted-noun keys (`kep_count`, `utvonal_count`, `korabbi_menet`, `offline_street_view_kesz_kep`, `offline_street_view_kep`, and `recent_count_utvonal`) have no `.one` form in English, so a count of 1 renders "1 images" or "1 routes". The STYLE rule requires both forms. Related: numeric placeholders next to a plural noun (`{climbs} climbs`, `{n} points fell outside`, `{rides} rides`) have no plural logic.
- Glossary gap for edzés: the glossary says edzés is "workout", but in the review page and the GPX/TCX import it means a recorded ride (rows 84-86, 692, 917, 928, 930, 931). The English "ride" there matches the source meaning and is correct, but the glossary has no row for this context, so future translators may flip it back.
- szintemelkedés: the glossary says "elevation gain", but the English uses "of climbing" in rows 96 (`app.controller.utvonal_betoltve_km_m_emelkedo`), 739, 740, 791 and 870, while rows 664 and 775 use "elevation gain" or "climbing left". Pick one term and apply it everywhere.
- Test keys: the `i18n.test.*` keys (rows 259-262) are in the English catalog, and two of them are empty (261, 262). Confirm they are fixtures that never reach the user-facing catalog.
